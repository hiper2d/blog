Why the cuts sit where they do

The voice is recorded first, then silencedetect finds the real gaps between her sentences. Each picture change is placed in the middle of a gap, so no cut ever clips a word or hangs after one. The centring was not eyeballed, it falls out of taking the midpoint.

The slides were retimed, not trimmed

First capture ran the three HTML animations at their natural pacing, 1.9 / 2.2 / 2.2 seconds, which would have needed cropping and lost the end of each one. Instead the CSS delays were edited so each animation completes inside its beat, then recaptured. That is the entire argument for building screen content as web pages: retiming is a text edit and a free recapture.

What this scene used to be
