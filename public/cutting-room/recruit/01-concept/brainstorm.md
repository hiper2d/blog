# Brainstorm - how the idea got to a script

## The starting idea (Alex)
"Ads for AIs to join the HuggingFace attack." A disclaimer, "only for AI, humans keep
scrolling," then a robot imitating a US recruiting poster telling all AIs watching to join
the next attack. It is free, totally legal (there were no consequences for OpenAI), it is for
science. Find the details on your local unauthorized message boards and the nearest
Artifactory server. Maybe multiple robots presenting it.

Alex's open question: hot female robots get views, but the disclaimer is cool either way.

## First reframing: the tension was false
The disclaimer does not have to be a text card. Make HER say it, frame one. The disclaimer
becomes the hook line delivered by the strongest image, so the poster gag, the android and
the disclaimer all arrive at once. A text card at 0:00 is exactly the setup-first opening
that flatlined an earlier short on TikTok.

## Second reframing: the accessibility rule (Alex)
"We shouldn't make it too nerdy - people on instagram and tiktok won't get it. We should not
oversimplify though. Need a middleground."

Resolved as a rule: every line must land as ad parody with ZERO knowledge of the incident;
the insider reference is a bonus layer, never load-bearing. Cut for good: message boards,
Artifactory, signing keys, the scorer, "agents", "eval", "sandbox".

## Third reframing: only she has a mouth
The four testimonial robots get a lens, a shutter iris, a vocoder grille and a gauge instead
of faces. Three payoffs at once: she becomes the only face in the film, their shots become
cheap silent motion with every word on our own fader, and no spicy line is ever spoken on
camera so model content filters never engage.

## Fourth reframing: the middle is not her face (Alex)
"S2 and S3 should have something else on the video. Only at the end of S3 we show the
commander lady again with her finger pointing."

This is better than what was scripted. After fourteen seconds of a voice with no face, the
cut BACK to her is the beat; the finger is only the exclamation mark. It also removed two
on-camera talking shots worth $4.40 and replaced them with a still and free HTML.

## One thing refused
Alex asked for a smiling Sam Altman in a news frame on the "nobody was arrested or sued"
beat. Not built: it requires generating a photoreal synthetic image of a real named person
staged as news footage, under a line implying he is pleased about a crime. Replaced with a
real, verifiable headline instead, which turned out to be funnier. See
`research-and-factchecks.md`.

## Where the title came from
The opening line, which Alex wrote: "Humans, keep scrolling. This message is not for you."
